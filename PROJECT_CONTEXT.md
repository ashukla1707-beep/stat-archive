## Project overview
Stat Archive (originally introduced as Project X) is a statistics educational-resource platform. The Books section provides catalog entries and, for some titles, full-PDF preview and offline access. The owner created the platform to make learning resources easier to find.

## Copyright concern (2026-10-10)
Some complete textbook PDFs were uploaded by the owner; some originated from official university websites. At least one book, *Statistical Inference* by George Casella and Roger L. Berger, was obtained from:
https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~shao/stat610/Casella_Berger_Statistical_Inference.pdf

The owner's Stat Archive preview serves a separately hosted copy (Google Drive or another server), rather than simply linking to that university URL. University availability is **not** evidence of third-party redistribution permission. Educational purpose alone does not automatically authorize public redistribution under Indian copyright law.

## Preliminary audit conclusions
- All nine need a license/permission check before Stat Archive hosts or distributes complete PDFs. Commercial textbook copies are potentially high-risk when redistributed without authorization.
- No redistribution permissions for these particular hosted copies have been verified.
- The university-hosted Casella–Berger PDF does not, by itself, grant permission to rehost it.
- *The Book of R* should not be assumed open-licensed merely because R software is open source.
- These are **provisional risk assessments**, not determinations of infringement. Confirm editions, publishers, licenses, sources and applicable legal exceptions.
- The connected Statistics Lover Dev Supabase database is a **different project**; its empty learning-resource tables do not establish the contents of Stat Archive.
