Reader User Manual

For students and read-only users · Updated August 2026

Who this manual is for: Students, readers and anyone who only needs to find and use study material.

Stat Archive organizes notes, books, previous-year questions, mid-term questions and other study material by course level and subject. Reader access does not require signing in.

1. Choose Your Course Level

  1. Use the M.Sc / B.Sc selector at the top of the archive.
  2. Choose your course level before browsing. Subjects and entries are level-specific.
  3. The site remembers your theme preference on the device.

2. Find Material Quickly

  • Search: Use the search box for title, filename, year or subject.
  • Subjects: Use subject filters to narrow the archive. On mobile, the subject controls stay compact and can be expanded when needed.
  • Types: Filter by Book, Notes, Previous Year Question, Mid-Term Question or Others.
  • Latest entries: Use Latest entries when you want recently added material first.
  • Subject rows: Entries are grouped by subject. Swipe or scroll horizontally within a subject row to see additional files.

3. Preview, Download and Status Pills

  1. Tap Preview to view supported files in the archive.
  2. Tap Download to save a copy to your device.
  3. When Preview or Download has been used on the device, its action pill can show a remembered used-state.
  4. Tap Offline in the installed app/PWA to save a local offline copy. A saved Offline action changes state so you can see that the file is already stored.
  5. Google Drive-linked large files open in Google Drive rather than using the normal local Preview/Download flow.
No account required: Browsing, filtering, previewing and downloading are reader features. Sign-in is only for archive maintenance roles.

4. Offline Library

  1. Open Offline Library from the installed Stat Archive app/PWA.
  2. The summary shows saved file count, subject count and local storage used.
  3. Search saved files by title, subject or year and filter them by subject/type.
  4. Saved files remain grouped by subject with horizontal browsing.
  5. Open offline opens the locally stored copy. Share sends the saved copy through the device sharing interface. Remove deletes only the local copy.
  6. The Offline Library header remains visible while the saved-file content scrolls.

5. Mobile and Installed-App Behaviour

  • The mobile layout keeps filters and summary information compact.
  • Dark and Light themes are supported.
  • When the app regains connectivity, archive data refreshes so newer material can load.
  • Opening a saved offline file and returning takes you back to the Offline Library.

6. Reader Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
A file will not previewTry Download, or Open in Drive if the entry is Drive-linked.
Offline button is unavailableUse the installed app/PWA rather than a normal browser tab.
New entries are missingReconnect to the internet and reopen/refresh Stat Archive.
Wrong subjects are shownCheck whether M.Sc or B.Sc is selected.
Layout looks unusualRefresh once and confirm you are using a current browser/app version.
